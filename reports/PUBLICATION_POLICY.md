# Publication Policy (decided 2026-08-15)

**1. Naming.** Public outputs (reports, README, any article) carry aggregates,
methodology, and anonymized patterns. Flag-attached supplier characterizations use
descriptors ("a facility-services group operating under three ΑΦΜs"), not names. Names
attached to findings are shared privately with oversight bodies (hospital administration,
2η ΥΠΕ, Υπουργείο Υγείας, or ΕΑΔ) on request. Names may appear publicly only where the
finding is verified at award-document level AND the finding survives right of reply.
Raw Diavgeia data in `data/` retains supplier names as-is: it is unaltered public record
republished for reproducibility — the policy governs our *characterizations*, not the
government's own published data.

**2. Right of reply.** Before publishing any findings report beyond this research
archive, the affected institutions receive a findings summary with a reasonable response
window (suggested: 15 working days). Responses are published alongside, verbatim or
summarized at the respondent's choice.

**3. Standing corrections.** Any error found in our analysis after publication is
corrected in-place with a dated retraction note (SENSE_CHECK.md discipline continues).

**Current status of named-supplier findings** (all resolved 2026-08-15, see
Fraud_Risk_Assessment §5): the three structuring flags closed as tendered contracts —
publishable without naming concern since the findings are exonerative. The single
remaining critical pattern (sole-bidder tender capture) is described without naming in
public text; the underlying ADAs are in the repo for anyone to verify independently.
