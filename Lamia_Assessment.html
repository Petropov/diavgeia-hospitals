Lamia: five insights from reading a city's entire public record

The town hall is not captured — it is casual, and its recurring money moves through channels built to avoid competition without ever breaking a rule. The hospital across town is where the euros and the anomalies actually concentrate. And the sharpest fixes cost almost nothing.

ΔΗΜΟΣ ΛΑΜΙΕΩΝ (6166) · ΓΝ ΛΑΜΙΑΣ (99221923) · 67,878 DECISIONS 2018–2026 · PDF-VERIFIED 2026 WINDOW · 10-MUNICIPALITY + 15-HOSPITAL BENCHMARKS · 2 JULY 2026

The insights, ranked by heat

1

The hospital is the referable file — seven-figure direct medical purchases inside a book built for anonymity

hottest

ΓΝ Λαμίας fires 8 of 9 automated fraud detectors — worst of 15 benchmarked hospitals (its size-twin, Serres, fires one). 64% of its awards publish no supplier identity; all 2,963 payment finalisations publish no amount. Verified in the residue after stripping misclassified records: €1.49M for osteosynthesis materials and €841k for neurosurgical supplies awarded by direct procedure, with more behind them.

So whatIf exception clauses fail scrutiny: contract invalidity and recovery claims. This is the file to put before the 5th Health Region and ΕΑΔ first — it outweighs everything municipal by an order of magnitude. Responsibility sits with the hospital's Governor and Board; the municipality has none.
2

The town hall's real leak is not the big contract — it's €864k dripped through 2,000+ micro-awards to eight vendors

hottest

Eight suppliers hold 195–474 direct awards each (2018–2026), €50–160k per relationship, zero tenders. Four vendor-years already exceed the €30,000 annual line — the worst: €50,841 to one vendor in 2025 across 66 separate awards. Every award is individually legal; every relationship is a divided annual contract. One of these vendors runs the same model in neighbouring Στυλίδα, where it is the top supplier.

So whatThis is where competitive savings die: at typical 5–15% competitive tension, €45–130k foregone. The remedy is boring and cheap — framework contracts for recurring supply. Until then, each new vendor-year over €30k is a potential disallowance at the Court of Audit.
3

Threshold engineering appeared in 2026 — twin contracts at €19,964.00 each, prices ending in "…999"

hot

Same contractor, same service, two districts, seven days apart, identical €19,964.00 — €39,928 combined, above the single-contract tender line (Ρ920ΩΛΚ-5ΓΙ, 9Ν52ΩΛΚ-3ΩΝ). Alongside: €14,999.77 (pool chemicals), €11,999.73 (printing). The historical archive shows no such bunching before 2026 — this is either new behaviour or newly visible, and the specifier of the tree works (a forester who also certifies the contractor's hours) is a standing annual incumbent alongside the contractor.

So whatThe clearest single Art. 6 / ν.4412 exposure at the municipality, with personal liability for authorising officers if confirmed. One document request settles it: the two cost estimates behind the twin awards. Signing authority: the Mayor and delegated deputy.
4

Opacity is the enabling condition — and it's an administrative choice, fixable for free

structural

One municipal committee published 63 of 63 vehicle-repair awards with blank supplier and amount fields — the data exists in the signed PDFs (we recovered it), it simply never reaches the searchable register. The hospital does the same at scale. Peer benchmarking shows the spectrum: Τρίκαλα publishes 94–97% complete and loses nothing by it; Καρδίτσα publishes essentially nothing (0% supplier IDs — across both its municipality and its hospital, a city-wide failure). Blank payment amounts are universal in all ten municipalities — a national platform norm, not a Lamia sin.

So whatEvery other finding in this brief was harder to establish because of this one. The fix costs nothing: fill the mandatory fields. Locally that's an instruction from the Mayor to one committee; nationally it's ΕΑΔ enforcement and a Ministry of Digital Governance validation rule (reject submissions with empty counterparty/amount on award types).
5

Context that keeps it honest: Lamia is mid-table, and the district's small towns are worse

counterweight

Against its four true size-peers, Lamia is unremarkable: lowest measured threshold-hugging (0.12% of priced awards, micro-skew caveat), middling identification (59%), best payment disclosure (3% vs 0% everywhere). No dominant vendor (top holds 4.4% of awards; carnival money spread across 13 local firms). The genuine outliers sit elsewhere: a 15k-resident town where one vendor holds 70% of all 3,157 awards (Καμένα Βούρλα), and hugging rates of 5–6% in Μακρακώμη, Δομοκός, Σέρρες.

So whatTwo implications. For Lamia: the story is "unguarded, not looted" — reforms are preventive, not punitive. For oversight policy: risk concentrates where scrutiny is thinnest — small municipalities and specific institutions (Καρδίτσα, Καμένα Βούρλα deserve this same treatment next, before Rhodes or anywhere else).

What would change this picture

Five open items, in order of impact: (1) Hospital ΑΝΑΘΕΣΗ re-classification — separates true awards from tender-procedure acts and re-sizes the largest exposure; (2) the two cost estimates behind the twin tree awards; (3) CPV-level confirmation of the four over-€30k vendor-years; (4) hydration of the municipal €10–30k band — decides whether threshold engineering is new or newly visible; (5) ΓΕΜΗ resolution of the eight atomized AFMs and the 70%-vendor of Καμένα Βούρλα. Items 1, 4, 5 run on the local pipeline (scripts ready); 2 is a records request; 3 is a day's data work.

Method. Complete populations, not samples: 67,878 municipal decisions (2018–2026); all Δ.1 direct awards 13 Feb–31 Mar 2026 re-fetched live and cross-checked against signed PDFs; 6,301 PDF-recovered historical amounts; 10-municipality and 15-hospital benchmarks. Forensic battery: threshold-cliff, Benford, identical-amount, same-subject clustering, vendor-year aggregation. Negative results reported (no historical threshold bunching; mild Benford deviation only).

Integrity note. Two earlier headline figures were retracted during this analysis after failing verification — a "€98M municipal spend" total and the hospital's "€47.7M / 28:1 direct-award ratio" — both contaminated by tender budgets recorded as spend in source tables. All euro figures above survive that audit. Facts are citable by ADA; nothing here asserts wrongdoing — each pattern carries a lawful explanation that only competent authorities (ΕΑΔ, Court of Audit) can weigh.
